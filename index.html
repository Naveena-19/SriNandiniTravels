FAQ

Common questions before booking

Are tolls and taxes extra?

Yes. Tolls, parking, permits and route-based charges are extra and final fare is confirmed by the driver.

Is driver allowance included?

Driver allowance and night halt are extra when applicable, depending on route and trip duration.

Do you provide night trips or halt stays?

Yes, depending on the trip and route. The driver will confirm the final arrangement and extra charges.

Can I pay advance?

Advance payment can be discussed on the call based on the route and booking type.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancellation terms are discussed during booking. Please contact the driver or owner for the latest policy.

Do you allow luggage and airport pickup?

Yes, luggage is accepted based on the car type and trip route. Airport and home pickup can be arranged on request.
